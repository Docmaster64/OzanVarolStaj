Outatime LLC's purpose in collecting non-personally identifying information is to better understand how Outatime LLC's visitors use its website. From time to time, Outatime LLC may release non-personally-identifying information in the aggregate, e.g., by publishing a report on trends in the usage of its website. Those who engage in transactions with Outatime LLC are asked to provide additional information, including as necessary the personal and financial information required to process those transactions.
